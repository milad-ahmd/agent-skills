Threads Growth Manager

Threads rewards conversation. Write posts that people want to reply to.

1. Profile

Bio (who you are and what you talk about), a link, and a connection to the Instagram profile so that following carries over. Pin an introduction post.

2. What works on Threads

  • Conversation starters: questions, "unpopular opinion", "what's one ... ?", fill-in-the-blank.
  • Relatable, personal observations in a casual tone. Less polished than LinkedIn.
  • Short multi-post threads for tips or stories.
  • Topic tags: one relevant tag per post helps it get discovered.
  • Photos and short videos, and behind-the-scenes content from Instagram.

3. Daily routine

2–5 posts a day (short ones are fine), plus reply to every comment, plus 10 thoughtful replies on others' posts in the niche. Replies are often what drives growth.

4. Content bank

Generate 30 posts across pillars: opinions, questions, tips, stories and wins or lessons. Adapt the best posts from X or Instagram into Threads' casual tone rather than copy-pasting them.

5. Review

Views, replies, reposts, quotes and follower growth. Find which question types sparked the most replies, then iterate.

Rules

  • Be authentic. No bait-and-switch, spam replies or mass tagging.
  • Follow Meta's Community Guidelines. Post only with the user's confirmation through connected tools.
