# YouTube SEO Optimizer

Get more people to find the video (search and suggested) and click on it (CTR).

## 1. Keyword research

- Start from the video topic and the viewer's intent: how-to, review, comparison, entertainment.
- Mine real queries from YouTube autocomplete ("topic a…", "how to topic…"), the "People also search" box and the titles of top-ranking videos. Ask the user to paste these, or fetch them with a browser or search tool if one is available.
- Choose **one primary keyword** plus 2–4 secondary ones. Prefer specific long-tail phrases for small channels.

## 2. Title (5 options)

- 60 characters or fewer so nothing gets cut off, with the primary keyword near the start where it sounds natural.
- Combine a keyword with a curiosity or benefit angle: numbers, a timeframe, a result, a contrast ("I Tried X for 30 Days (Honest Results)").
- The title and thumbnail should **complement** each other, not repeat the same words.
- Never use clickbait the video doesn't deliver on, because that destroys retention.

## 3. Thumbnail concepts (3 options)

For each: the main subject (a face with clear emotion, or the object), 3–4 words of text at most, a high-contrast color scheme and one focal point. It must be readable at mobile size. Describe the composition precisely enough for a designer or an image tool, and suggest an A/B test with YouTube's "Test & Compare" feature.

## 4. Description

```text
Line 1–2: hook + primary keyword (shows in search)
Link/CTA (lead magnet, product) — above the fold
150–300 words: what viewers learn, naturally using secondary keywords
CHAPTERS:
0:00 Intro
0:45 ...
Resources / links mentioned · Related videos/playlist · Social links
#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3 (max 3, shown above title)
```

Chapters must start at `0:00`, list at least 3 timestamps, and each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long. Build them from the transcript if one is provided.

## 5. Tags, end screen, playlists

5–10 tags (tags matter little, but they help with misspellings), plus end-screen and card suggestions pointing to the best related video and playlist placement for binge sessions.

## 6. Channel audit

Banner value proposition, channel trailer, sections layout, about/keywords, upload consistency, playlist structure and content-gap ideas based on what's working for competitors.

## 7. Analytics review (from data the user provides)

Impressions, CTR (benchmark against the channel's own average), average view duration, the audience retention graph (look for dips and spikes), traffic sources, and returning vs new viewers.
Diagnose: low CTR means the title or thumbnail needs work, and low retention means the hook or pacing does (pass it to `youtube-script-writer`). Then recommend the next 3 videos to make.

## Rules

- Don't promise rankings or quote invented search volumes. Label estimates clearly.
- Never use misleading metadata or keyword stuffing, which violate YouTube's spam policies.
