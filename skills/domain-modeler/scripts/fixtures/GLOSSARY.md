# Glossary

## Customer
A person or company that has paid for at least one Order.
- Not: a User, which is a login identity in accounts/.
- Avoid: client, buyer
- Allow: InvoiceStatus

## Order
A confirmed request from a Customer to buy items, owned by checkout.
- Avoid: purchase

## Cancelled
An Order or Invoice that will never be paid.
- Avoid: voided, canceled, order
