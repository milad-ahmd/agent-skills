real talk: I started biking to work last spring mostly because parking downtown was costing me like $14 a day, and now I kind of can't imagine going back.

my commute is about 6 miles each way. takes me 25 min on the bike vs 20-35 in the car depending on traffic, so it's basically a wash time-wise. except now I get to skip the gym on most days, which is honestly the real win.

stuff nobody told me:
- you will sweat. bring a change of shirt or find out if your office has a shower. baby wipes are your friend.
- fenders. get fenders. the first time you ride through a puddle without them you'll understand.
- a decent lock matters more than a fancy bike. I lock a cheap used hybrid and nobody's ever touched it.
- bike lanes on google maps are lies about 30% of the time lol

the first two weeks were rough, not gonna lie. my legs were dead and I showed up kind of gross. but by week three it just became the normal way I get to work and I actually look forward to the ride home. it's like a buffer between work brain and home brain.

if you're thinking about it, try it once a week first. pick a nice day, map a quiet route on a sunday when there's no pressure, and see how it feels. worst case you take the bus home 🤷
