Handwashing is one of the simplest yet most effective ways to prevent the spread of germs and protect your health. While it may seem like a basic habit, washing your hands properly can significantly reduce the risk of illnesses such as the common cold, flu, and foodborne infections.

**When Should You Wash Your Hands?**

It's especially important to wash your hands:
- Before, during, and after preparing food
- Before eating
- After using the restroom
- After blowing your nose, coughing, or sneezing
- After touching animals or handling garbage

**How to Wash Your Hands Properly**

1. **Wet** your hands with clean, running water.
2. **Lather** with soap, making sure to cover the backs of your hands, between your fingers, and under your nails.
3. **Scrub** for at least 20 seconds. A helpful tip is to hum the "Happy Birthday" song twice.
4. **Rinse** thoroughly under running water.
5. **Dry** your hands with a clean towel or air dryer.

**What About Hand Sanitizer?**

When soap and water aren't available, an alcohol-based hand sanitizer containing at least 60% alcohol can be a convenient alternative. However, it's worth noting that sanitizers are less effective on visibly dirty or greasy hands and don't eliminate all types of germs.

By making proper handwashing a regular part of your daily routine, you can play an active role in protecting not only yourself but also your family and community. It's a small habit that makes a big difference!
