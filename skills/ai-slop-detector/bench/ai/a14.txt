so you wanna run a marathon?? love that for you. ok here's what I wish someone had told me before my first one.

first, it's 26.2 miles and the last 6 are a completely different race. everyone says this and you won't believe it until mile 20 when your legs just kind of file a complaint and stop cooperating. that's "the wall." it's real. it's mostly your body running low on stored glycogen, which is why people eat those weird gel packets during the race.

training is usually 16-20 weeks. most plans have you running 4-5 days a week with one long run on the weekend that slowly gets longer, topping out around 18-20 miles a few weeks before race day. then you "taper," which means you cut back so your legs are fresh. tapering feels illegal. do it anyway.

couple things that actually matter:
- go slower than you think on long runs. like, embarrassingly slow. you should be able to talk.
- practice your race-day breakfast and gels during training. do NOT try anything new on race day (ask me how I know 💀)
- buy shoes at a running store where they watch you run. worth it.

and honestly? the finish line is kind of emotional in a way I didn't expect. strangers cheering for you for four hours straight does something to your brain.

you got this. start with a half if you've never done one, though. trust me.
