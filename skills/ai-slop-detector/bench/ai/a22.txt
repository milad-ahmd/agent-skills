🌀 **Hurricane Preparedness Checklist: Stay Safe and Ready** 🌀

Hurricane season can be stressful, but with the right preparation, you can protect your family, home, and peace of mind. Here's a comprehensive guide to help you get ready before a storm hits.

📋 **Before the Storm**

- ✅ **Know your zone:** Find out if you live in an evacuation zone and plan your route in advance.
- ✅ **Build an emergency kit:** Include water (one gallon per person per day for at least three days), non-perishable food, flashlights, batteries, medications, and a first-aid kit.
- ✅ **Secure important documents:** Store insurance policies, IDs, and medical records in a waterproof container or digital backup.
- ✅ **Protect your home:** Install storm shutters, trim trees, and bring in outdoor furniture.

📱 **Stay Informed**

- Sign up for local emergency alerts.
- Keep a battery-powered or hand-crank radio on hand.
- Follow updates from the National Hurricane Center.

🏠 **During the Storm**

- Stay indoors and away from windows.
- If flooding occurs, move to the highest level of your home, but avoid closed attics.
- Never walk or drive through floodwaters. Just six inches of moving water can knock you off your feet.

🔧 **After the Storm**

- Wait for officials to declare the area safe before returning home.
- Watch out for downed power lines and damaged structures.
- Document any damage with photos for insurance claims.

Remember, preparation is key! Taking these steps now can make all the difference when a hurricane strikes. Stay safe, stay prepared, and look out for your neighbors. 💪
