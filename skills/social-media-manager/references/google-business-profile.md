# Google Business Profile (Google Maps)

Local SEO through the Google Business Profile: categories, description, services and products, photos, posts, reviews and replies, NAP consistency and Performance insights.

## Platform facts

| Item | Limit / note |
|---|---|
| Categories | 1 primary + up to 9 additional |
| Description | 750 characters. No links, no promotional claims like "best price" |
| Business name | Must match the real-world name on signage and documents. Adding keywords or locations can get the profile suspended |
| Posts | Updates, Offers (dates, terms, coupon code) and Events, each up to 1,500 characters with an image and a button |
| Address | Service-area businesses hide the address and list areas served. Virtual offices, PO boxes and coworking desks without staff aren't allowed |
| Messaging | Google's chat feature (Business Messages) was shut down in 2024. Calls, website and booking links remain |
| Q&A | Google has been phasing out the Q&A section. If it's gone for the user, put common answers in the description, services and website |
| Suggested edits | Google and the public can change profile details. Check the profile for Google updates regularly |

## How visibility works

- Google says local results are based on **relevance** (how well the profile matches the search), **distance** and **prominence** (reviews, links, how well-known the business is).
- Local SEO practitioners widely treat the primary category as the strongest controllable factor. Pick the most specific accurate one.
- Review count, rating, recency and replies feed prominence and help people choose.
- A complete, accurate profile with current hours and fresh photos is the baseline.

## Intake (save to `social/google-business.md`)

- Business name, address or service area, phone, website, booking link.
- Owner or manager access to the profile? Verification status?
- Main services or products and the customers they serve. Top competitors in the area.
- Current rating and review count, how reviews are requested today.
- Other directories the business is listed in.

## Deliverables

**Profile audit** (score each item and fix list): primary and secondary categories, exact business name, address or service area, regular and holiday hours, phone, website with UTM tags, booking link, description (what, for whom, where, what's different, keywords used naturally), services and products with descriptions and prices, attributes, opening date, photos (logo, cover, exterior, interior, team, products or work) with a plan to add new ones regularly.

**Posts**: 4 weeks of updates, offers (with dates and terms) and events, each with an image and a CTA button.

**Review system**
- Request process: right after a good experience, with the profile's short review link or QR code, and templates for SMS, email and WhatsApp.
- Reply templates: personal, specific and timely. Negative reviews: thank, apologise for the experience, share no private details, take it offline, resolve it. Never argue.
- Flagging: how to report reviews that break Google's policies (spam, conflicts of interest, off-topic). Bad but genuine reviews can't be removed.

**Local SEO outside the profile**: NAP (name, address, phone) consistency across Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp and local directories; location pages on the website with LocalBusiness schema; local backlinks (partners, local press, associations).

**Performance review**: searches and the queries used, profile views on Search vs Maps, calls, direction requests, website clicks and bookings. Then next actions.

## Policy and ToS

- Google's review policies ban fake reviews, incentives for reviews (discounts, gifts) and review gating (asking only happy customers). Breaking them can remove reviews or restrict the profile.
- No keyword stuffing in the name, no virtual offices, one profile per real location.
- Changing the name, address or category can trigger re-verification. Warn the user before suggesting those edits.

## Tools and posting

- The user's own Business Profile access (Search, Maps or the Business Profile manager).
- Business Profile APIs need an approved Google Cloud project. Multi-location tools (BrightLocal, Yext and others) use them.
- Draft everything; update the profile, publish posts or reply to reviews only through the user's own access or connected tools, after they confirm.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Name matches real-world signage exactly
- [ ] Primary category is the most specific accurate one
- [ ] Hours, including holiday hours, are current
- [ ] Description has no links or unsupported claims
- [ ] Review requests go to all customers, with no incentive
- [ ] Review replies share no private details
- [ ] User has approved before any change goes live
