# Facebook (Pages and Groups)

Running a Facebook Page and Groups for a brand or local business: setup and audit, post and Reels copy, Group moderation, Messenger replies, events, recommendations, boosting and Insights reviews.

## Platform facts

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Page categories | Up to 3. The first one shapes how Facebook describes the Page |
| CTA button | One per Page: Book, Shop, Message, Call, WhatsApp, Sign up and others |
| Reviews | Shown as Recommendations (recommend / don't recommend plus text). Owners can reply and report policy violations but can't delete them |
| Group membership questions | Up to 3, plus an option to make members agree to the rules |
| Messenger (API) | Standard 24-hour window to reply after the person's last message. After that, only limited message tags are allowed |
| Reels | Facebook pushes short vertical video heavily, including to non-followers |

## How visibility works

- Organic Page reach to followers is low. Reels and posts that get shared, commented on and saved are the main way to reach new people.
- Groups often get more engagement than Pages because members choose to be there and get notified.
- Meaningful comments and shares count for more than reactions. Engagement bait ("comment YES", "tag 3 friends") is demoted by Facebook's ranking.
- Links in posts tend to reach fewer people than native content. Test link-in-comment if traffic matters.

## Intake (save to `social/facebook.md`)

- Business type, location(s), offer, ideal customer.
- Page URL, Group URL if any, current followers and admins.
- Goals: messages, bookings, store visits, online sales, community.
- Who answers Messenger and when. Existing FAQ answers (prices, hours, delivery, booking).
- Ad budget, if any.

## Deliverables

**Page audit and setup**: name, categories, profile and cover image (offer and CTA visible), intro and About, contact details, hours, location or service area, CTA button, username, Shop / Services tabs, links to Instagram and WhatsApp.

**Content plan** (3–5 posts a week)
- Reels (see `tiktok-viral-creator` for script structure).
- Photo posts and albums: products, behind the scenes, customers (only with their permission).
- Community posts: questions, polls, "this or that", local news and events.
- Offers: clear terms and a real deadline.
- Live: Q&A, launches, demos.
- Copy: hook in the first line, conversational, one CTA, tested links.

**Group setup**: purpose and name, 5–7 rules, membership questions, welcome post, weekly rituals (Monday wins, Friday feedback), moderation guidelines, spam handling, admin and moderator roles.

**Messenger and comments**: templates for FAQs, complaints (acknowledge, move to private, resolve) and lead qualification. Saved replies and an away message in Meta Business Suite. Aim for replies within an hour in business hours.

**Recommendations**: a process to ask happy customers. Reply to every one: thank positive ones specifically, answer negative ones calmly and factually with an offer to resolve it privately.

**Boosting**: boost only posts that already did well organically, with a clear objective, audience (location, interests, engagers), budget and end date. Anything more complex goes to Ads Manager (see `ad-copy-generator`).

**Insights review**: reach, engagement, Reels plays, follower growth, messages, link clicks, top posts and why, next actions.

## Policy and ToS

- Promotions on Pages (Pages, Groups and Events Policies): you can't require people to share on their timeline or tag friends to enter. The promotion must include a release of Meta and say it isn't sponsored or run by Facebook. Follow local contest laws.
- Ads in special categories (housing, employment, credit, social issues, elections or politics) have restricted targeting and extra authorisation. Flag this before planning a boost.
- Paid partnerships with creators use the branded content tool.
- Follow the Community Standards. Never fake reviews or engagement. Don't reveal customers' personal details in public replies.

## Tools and posting

- Meta Business Suite for scheduling posts and Reels, the shared inbox (Facebook, Messenger, Instagram) and Insights.
- The Graph API (Pages API, Messenger Platform) for custom tools. Needs an app with the right permissions.
- Draft everything; publish, reply or boost only through the user's connected tools, after they confirm.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Page info (hours, contact, CTA) is complete and current
- [ ] Each post has one clear CTA and a working link
- [ ] No engagement bait or share-to-enter mechanics
- [ ] Replies don't expose customer details
- [ ] Boost has an objective, audience, budget and end date, and isn't a special-category ad by accident
- [ ] User has approved before anything is published or sent
