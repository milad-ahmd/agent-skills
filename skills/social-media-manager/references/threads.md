# Threads (Meta)

Meta's Threads app (threads.com, formerly threads.net): profile setup, conversation-starting posts, multi-post threads, topic tags, reply engagement, cross-posting from X and Instagram, and reviews.

## Platform facts

| Item | Limit / note |
|---|---|
| Post | 500 characters. Longer content goes in a multi-post thread |
| Topic tag | One per post. Tags help a post get discovered by people interested in that topic |
| Media | Photos, carousels and short videos |
| Profile | Historically created from, and tied to, an Instagram account. Bio and followers can carry over |
| Fediverse | Optional sharing so people on Mastodon and other fediverse apps can follow |

## How visibility works

- The feed mixes followed accounts with recommendations, so new accounts can reach non-followers early.
- Replies and conversation drive reach. Posts that make people want to answer do better than polished announcements.
- Being active in other people's replies is a major growth source, because good replies get seen by that post's audience.
- The tone is casual. Copy-pasted LinkedIn or X posts often feel off.

## Intake (save to `social/threads.md`)

- Is there an Instagram account to connect? Current Threads followers?
- Niche, audience, goal, and voice (link 3 past posts from any platform).
- Content that already works on X or Instagram and could be adapted.
- Time per day for posting and replying.

## Deliverables

**Profile**: bio (who you are, what you talk about), link, connection to Instagram, pinned intro post.

**What to post**
- Conversation starters: questions, "unpopular opinion", "what's one…?", fill-in-the-blank.
- Relatable personal observations in a casual tone.
- Short multi-post threads for tips or stories.
- One relevant topic tag per post.
- Photos, short video and behind-the-scenes from Instagram.

**Daily routine**: several short posts a day is common on Threads (test 2–5), reply to every comment, plus about 10 thoughtful replies on others' posts in the niche.

**Content bank**: 30 posts across opinions, questions, tips, stories and wins or lessons. Adapt the best X or Instagram posts into Threads' tone rather than pasting them.

**Review**: views, replies, reposts, quotes and follower growth. Which question types got the most replies, then iterate.

## Policy and ToS

- Threads follows Instagram's Community Guidelines and Meta's terms.
- No bait-and-switch, spam replies, mass tagging or follow-for-follow.
- Disclose paid partnerships.

## Tools and posting

- Native scheduling and drafts in the Threads app.
- The Threads API supports publishing, reply management and insights, with a daily publishing cap. Buffer, Hootsuite and similar tools use it.
- Draft everything; post only through the user's connected tools, after they confirm.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Under 500 characters per post
- [ ] Invites a reply, not just a like
- [ ] One relevant topic tag
- [ ] Tone is casual and adapted, not pasted from another platform
- [ ] User has approved before anything is posted
