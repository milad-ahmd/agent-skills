# Telegram Channel Manager

Run a Telegram channel people keep unmuted.

## 1. Setup

Channel name and @username (short and searchable), a clear description (what, how often, for whom), an avatar, a pinned welcome post (what to expect, links, rules), a linked discussion group for comments, and admin roles.

## 2. Content strategy

- One clear promise ("daily AI tools in 3 minutes"). Consistency beats volume, and 1–3 posts a day is typical.
- A mix of formats: short insight posts, curated links with commentary, original images and infographics, short videos and voice notes, polls and quizzes, and exclusive content (a reason to stay subscribed).
- Plan a weekly calendar of recurring series ("Monday Tools", "Friday Recap").

## 3. Post formatting

Bold headline, short paragraphs, emoji bullets in moderation, a clear CTA, and link previews only when they're useful. Use Telegram formatting (**bold**, _italic_, `code`, spoilers) and silent posts for late hours. Write in the channel's language. Offer bilingual versions if the audience is mixed.

## 4. Groups and moderation

Rules (5–7), an anti-spam setup (slow mode, join requests, captcha or admin bots), FAQ replies, and an escalation process. Reply templates for common questions.

## 5. Growth

Cross-promotion with similar-sized channels (shoutout swaps), links from other platforms and the website, invite links tracked per source, valuable content people forward, and giveaways that follow local laws.

## 6. Monetization (if wanted)

Sponsored posts (always labelled), paid subscriptions or premium groups, affiliate links (disclosed), your own products and Telegram Stars or Ads revenue where available.

## 7. Analytics

Subscribers growth and churn, views per post vs subscriber count (view rate), forwards, reactions and the top posts. Then decide what to post more of and what to stop.

## Rules

- No buying subscribers or fake views. Label ads clearly.
- Respect privacy, and never expose group members' personal data.
- Post or schedule only through the user's connected bot or tools, with their confirmation.
